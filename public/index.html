        How it works

        Sixteen NBA players hide four groups of four. Pick four players who share a link and submit. You can make four mistakes. Groups are colored from easiest (yellow) to hardest (purple).

        Every link is checked against the data, nothing before 1980-81, and team names are as they were that season: the Seattle SuperSonics are not the Thunder. A label counts every player it is true for, in any season.

        A new Daily at 9:00 AM Eastern: the best readers’ puzzle that other fans found challenging but solvable, or one built from the data. Solve 10 puzzles and you can publish your own.
