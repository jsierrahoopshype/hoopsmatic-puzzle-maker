# Draft for hoopsmatic.com/privacy: Puzzle Maker

> **Puzzle Maker.** When you play, we store your guesses, how many mistakes you made, how long you took and whether you solved the puzzle, so we can show solve rates, rank today's leaderboard, rate puzzles and their makers and pick the Daily. When you publish a puzzle, we store its title, its 16 players and four groups, and the maker name you choose to show (if any). To tell your plays and puzzles apart from other people's, your browser keeps a random ID that we create. We store only a scrambled form of it (a keyed hash), which cannot be turned back into the ID or linked to you. We do not ask for or store your email or an account, and we do not store your IP address. To limit abuse, we count puzzles published per network with a code that changes every day and is deleted after two days. Puzzle titles, maker names and leaderboard names pass a word filter before they are shown. Plays and leaderboard entries are kept for up to 400 days and then deleted, except each day's top 50 named leaderboard entries. Published puzzles stay up until you ask us, or we decide, to remove them. A puzzle's totals (plays, solve rate) contain nothing about any one person.
